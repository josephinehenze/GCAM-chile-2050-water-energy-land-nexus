# Decarbonizing under Water Stress: Water-Energy-Land Nexus Insights from Chile's 2050 Carbon Neutrality Pathway

This repository contains the output data files associated with the publication. The data covers nine long-term energy-land-water scenarios for Chile's transition to carbon neutrality by 2050, with a particular focus on the interactions between carbon capture and storage (CCS), renewable energy deployment, and land-based mitigation under conditions of water stress.

---

## Repository contents

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Aggregated_land_CCS.csv` | Aggregated land use outcomes across all regions and sectors for each scenario |
| `Crop_production_CCS.csv` | Crop production levels by type and region under each scenario |
| `Elec_Gen_Tech_32reg_CCS.csv` | Electricity generation by technology across 32 regional nodes for each scenario |
| `Elec_Gen_Tech_cooling_tech_CCS.csv` | Electricity generation disaggregated by cooling technology type, relevant to water withdrawal and consumption |
| `Final_Energy_Consumption_Fuel_CCS.csv` | Final energy consumption broken down by fuel type across sectors and scenarios |
| `LUC_CCS.csv` | Land use change (LUC) emissions and transitions for each scenario |
| `NonCO2_region_CCS.csv` | Non-CO₂ greenhouse gas emissions by region and scenario |
| `PECDE_CCS.csv` | Primary energy consumption and decarbonization effort by energy carrier and scenario |
| `Water_wdw_sector_CCS.csv` | Water withdrawals by sector, capturing demand-side water stress implications across scenarios |

---

## Scenarios

The nine scenarios represent combinations of key decarbonization strategies — renewable energy, carbon capture and storage, and land-based mitigation — to explore trade-offs across the water-energy-land nexus.

| Code | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| `REF` | Reference | Baseline trajectory without additional climate policy interventions |
| `RE` | Renewable Energy Focus | Decarbonization driven primarily by large-scale renewable energy deployment |
| `CCS` | Carbon Capture & Storage Focus | Decarbonization driven primarily by carbon capture and storage technologies |
| `RE + LLM` | Renewable Energy + Limited Land Mitigation | Renewable energy strategy with a limited contribution from land-based mitigation measures |
| `CCS + LLM` | CCS + Limited Land Mitigation | CCS strategy combined with limited land-based mitigation |
| `RE + MLM` | Renewable Energy + Moderate Land Mitigation | Renewable energy strategy with a moderate contribution from land-based mitigation |
| `CCS + MLM` | CCS + Moderate Land Mitigation | CCS strategy combined with moderate land-based mitigation |
| `RE + HLM` | Renewable Energy + High Land Mitigation | Renewable energy strategy with a high contribution from land-based mitigation |
| `CCS + HLM` | CCS + High Land Mitigation | CCS strategy combined with high land-based mitigation |
